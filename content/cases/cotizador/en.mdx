## Context

## My role

I led UX/UI design and analysis end to end: stakeholder interviews, requirements, the quoting flow, interface design, QA and rollout.

## Iterating until the system and the design agree

The quoting tool went through seven versions. Each one was checked against what the system actually did, until we reached an approved version for Peru. Then came a freight submodule and the migration of the whole module to the ERP's design system.
