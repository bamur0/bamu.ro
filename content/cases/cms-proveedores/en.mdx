## Context

Suppliers needed to update their product catalog without depending on anyone else. I led the design and development of a CMS from scratch so those updates would show up in real time.

<Pending>
How did they update the catalog before (spreadsheets, email, support)? How long did it take? How many suppliers and products does it handle today?
</Pending>

## My role

I owned the full product cycle: research, user flow, design system, interface design, prototype and user testing.

## Structure before screens

Before any screens I mapped the supplier's full flow and the catalog's product families, along with a spec for every data field. That map shaped how product creation and change history were structured.

<PendingVisual label="User flow and product family mapping" />

## What was designed

Sign-in and its scenarios (including concurrent sessions), terms of use, dashboard, catalog, product creation and editing, change history, loading states and error pages. All of it in three resolutions, from 1366 to 2560 px, plus a clickable prototype for testing.
